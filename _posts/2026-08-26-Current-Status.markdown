## Experimental Physics Studying Neutrinos

#### Collection of Neutrino Experiment on Earth

[Neutrino Experiment on Earth](https://ooohu.github.io/NeutrinoExperimentsOnEarth/) is a webpage to show all the neutrino detectors that I have ever know. 
It is not a trivial task to accurately pin-point where they are, but it is an interesting project where physicists can help contribute their insight of how to locate their beloved detectors on earth.


#### Interactive SBND CRT Map
[This Map](https://github.com/Ooohu/SBND_CRT_InteractiveMap)
records the long list of the front end boards of where they are located in the SBND detector. With this map, a CRT panel is no longer just a number, but it is a piece of device whose location relative to the LArTPC can be visualized.


#### 3D Printed Scaled-down Detectors
Using Siemens NX and Prusa Slicer, the engineering blueprints can be converted into a miniature. A meter-scale detector can become a portable cm-scale model without losing fine details.
