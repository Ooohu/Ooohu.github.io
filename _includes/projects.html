I am an experimental physics PhD candidate at Rutgers University, specializing in neutrino experiments. Neutrinos are the most elusive particles ever discovered in our universe, and they barely interact with matters. At Fermilab, neutrinos can be produced using accelerators and be studied using Liquid Argon Time Projection Chambers (LArTPCs). My researches combine large-scale detector commissioning, data analysis, simulation, and machine learning to search for rare signals in two LArTPCs: MicroBooNE and SBND.
